# ⚔️ ECHO DUNGEON

**A dark fantasy 2D action roguelite built with Unity 6 and C#.**

**Status:** In Active Development — Playable Prototype

## 🎮 About the Game

ECHO DUNGEON is a top-down action roguelite focused on satisfying combat, different weapon playstyles, and challenging dungeon encounters.

Fight through five combat rooms, choose upgrades, discover weapons, and face **The Warden** in the final encounter.

The project is currently in development. Gameplay balance, animations, and visuals are still being improved.

## ⚔️ Current Features

### Combat & Weapons
- **Sword:** Fast melee combos, throwing dagger, Blade Rush, and Heavy Slash.
- **Two-Handed Axe:** Heavy combos, Crushing Blow, Ground Slam, and Whirlwind.
- Hit stop, knockback, impact effects, and cooldown-based abilities.
- A weapon chest that allows players to keep their current weapon or equip a new one.

### Enemies & Dungeon
- Three enemy types: Grunt, Brute, and Archer.
- Obstacle-aware enemy navigation.
- Five normal combat rooms.
- A multi-phase boss encounter against The Warden.
- Upgrade choices during each run.

### Echo Rebirth
Death does not always mean the end.

Players have one opportunity per run to return to battle through **Echo Rebirth**, keeping the current encounter's progress.
